Extract the final stress–strain data for each configuration, and fit Cij: `σ_i(k) = Σ_j C_ij · ε_j(k)`. Use the standard sign convention — positive σ = tension; QE prints stress with the opposite (compression-positive) sign, so negate it accordingly. A correct stiffness tensor for a stable crystal is positive-definite (all eigenvalues > 0); a negative-definite result means the sign was not flipped.
